Newham Community Learning - Primary Schools KS2 Results 2023/24

This report presents the 2023/24 KS2 results for NCL's two primary schools (Portway Primary School and Selwyn Primary School) plus Carpenters Primary School, for which NCL serves as school improvement partner. Data is from the DfE Compare Schools service, published April 2025. All comparisons include Local Authority (Newham) and England averages.

Context & Key Observations

  • Performance Range: Combined Reading, Writing & Maths at expected standard ranges from 62% (Carpenters) to 77% (Portway), spanning 15 percentage points
  • Portway Excellence: Portway (77%) outperforms both LA average (74%) and England average (61%), demonstrating strong outcomes across the board
  • Selwyn Solid Performance: Selwyn (72%) performs slightly below LA average but significantly above England average, showing consistent strength
  • Carpenters Challenge: Carpenters (62%) requires support - higher standard attainment at 4% is significantly below LA (13%) and England (8%) averages
  • Higher Standard Performance: Both Portway (23%) and Selwyn (22%) significantly exceed England average (8%) and LA average (13%) for higher standard attainment
  • Scaled Scores Strength: Portway and Selwyn both achieve scaled scores of 108 in Reading and Maths, above LA (107) and England (105/104) averages
  • Greater Depth Gap at Carpenters: Carpenters' 4% higher standard rate suggests need for targeted support for higher-attaining pupils

Strategic Recommendations

  1. Share Portway Best Practice: Establish formal mechanisms to share Portway's successful strategies (77% expected, 23% higher) across NCL primaries and with Carpenters, particularly in developing higher-attaining pupils.
  2. Carpenters Targeted Support: Provide intensive school improvement support to Carpenters, focusing on greater depth teaching and strategies to stretch higher-attaining pupils (currently 4% vs 13% LA average).
  3. Maintain Selwyn Strength: Continue to support Selwyn's strong performance (72% expected, 22% higher) while exploring opportunities to close the 5-point gap with Portway at expected standard.
  4. Greater Depth Network: Create cross-school moderation and planning sessions for Year 6 teachers to share approaches for developing pupils working at greater depth, leveraging Portway and Selwyn's success.
  5. Carpenters Writing Focus: Investigate specific barriers to combined RWM attainment at Carpenters - likely writing is the limiting factor given reading and maths scaled scores at 105.
  6. Year 6 Transition: Ensure effective transition arrangements between NCL primaries and secondaries, using KS2 data to inform KS3 intervention planning (noting Progress 8 challenges at secondary level).
  7. Assessment and Moderation: Strengthen trust-wide assessment practices and writing moderation across all three schools to ensure consistency and accuracy of teacher assessment.
Important Note - Data Timeline: This primary schools data is from the 2023/24 academic year (published April 2025), making it one year older than the secondary schools data which covers 2024/25 (published October 2025). When comparing primary and secondary performance, consider this timing difference.
* Partnership School: Carpenters Primary School is not part of the Newham Community Learning Trust. NCL serves as the school improvement partner for Carpenters, providing strategic support and guidance to improve outcomes.
