Newham Community Learning - Primary Schools Absence & Demographics

This report presents absence data from 2023/24 and demographic data from 2024/25 for NCL's two primary schools (Portway Primary School and Selwyn Primary School) plus Carpenters Primary School, for which NCL serves as school improvement partner. Data is from the DfE Compare Schools service. All comparisons include Local Authority (Newham) and England averages where available.

Context & Key Observations

  • Persistent Absence Challenge: All three schools exceed England average (14.6%) for persistent absence, with Selwyn at 22.9%, Carpenters at 21.6%, and Portway at 19.8%
  • Overall Absence Above National: Portway (6.2%), Carpenters (6.5%) and Selwyn (6.9%) all exceed England average (5.5%), though close to LA average (6%)
  • High EAL Context: All three schools serve significantly higher proportions of EAL pupils than national average (23.4%) - Selwyn 83.10%, Carpenters 70.10%, Portway 68.70%
  • Elevated Deprivation: FSM eligibility ranges from 37.90% (Portway) to 45.64% (Carpenters), all substantially above England average (26.3%)
  • SEN Variation: Carpenters (21.30% total SEN) and Selwyn (20.13% total SEN) exceed national averages, while Portway (11.88%) is below
  • Small School Context: Carpenters (338 pupils) and Selwyn (462 pupils) are smaller than Portway (547 pupils), which may impact statistical volatility
  • Gender Balance: All three schools show relatively balanced gender distribution, close to national averages

Strategic Recommendations

  1. Persistent Absence Action Plan: Develop trust-wide strategy to address persistent absence, which exceeds national average across all schools. Focus on early intervention, family engagement, and barriers analysis. Share successful approaches from Portway (lowest at 19.8%).
  2. Carpenters Intensive Support: Provide targeted support to Carpenters given highest FSM (45.64%), elevated SEN (21.30% combined), and persistent absence (21.6%). Consider additional pastoral resources and family support services.
  3. Selwyn Attendance Focus: Priority intervention needed at Selwyn where persistent absence (22.9%) is highest across all three schools, despite strong KS2 attainment outcomes.
  4. EAL Excellence Sharing: Leverage the strong EAL practice evident across all schools (68-83% EAL vs 23.4% national) as a trust strength. Document and share effective strategies for supporting multilingual learners.
  5. SEN Provision Review: Investigate variation in SEN identification and support across schools - Portway 11.88% total SEN vs Carpenters 21.30%. Ensure consistency in identification processes and intervention quality.
  6. Attendance and Attainment Analysis: Examine correlation between attendance patterns and KS2 outcomes. Carpenters' combination of high absence and lower attainment suggests this as a key improvement lever.
  7. Deprivation-Responsive Practice: With FSM rates 11-19 points above national average, ensure all schools have robust pupil premium strategies, targeted interventions, and appropriate pastoral support systems.
  8. Cross-School Learning: Establish regular forums for attendance leads and pastoral teams to share strategies, particularly around engaging families from diverse linguistic and cultural backgrounds.
Important Notes:
  • Absence data is from academic year 2023/24
  • Demographic data is from academic year 2024/25 (current year)
  • These schools serve communities with significantly higher levels of deprivation and linguistic diversity than national averages
  • Small school context (particularly Carpenters at 338 pupils) means percentages can be volatile year-on-year
* Partnership School: Carpenters Primary School is not part of the Newham Community Learning Trust. NCL serves as the school improvement partner for Carpenters, providing strategic support and guidance to improve outcomes.
