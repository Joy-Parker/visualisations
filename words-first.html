Procurement Requirements Checklist

Words First Limited - Multidisciplinary Therapeutic Services
Selwyn Primary School | Contract Value: £91,105

Requirements How these were met
Effective professional support and expertise was secured The Project Consultant has reviewed how this service has been procured, at the instruction of the CEO. Words First Limited is an established provider of multidisciplinary therapeutic services to schools, offering integrated professional support across multiple therapeutic disciplines.
Scope of service requirements clearly understood The service encompasses a comprehensive package of therapeutic interventions tailored to the identified needs of Selwyn Primary School students:
  • Speech & Language Therapy: 4 days per week for 41 weeks
  • Educational Psychology: 24 days total
  • Counselling: 1 day per week for 41 weeks
  • Resource bundle: Included at no charge
This coordinated multidisciplinary approach ensures holistic support for students with diverse therapeutic needs.
Costs of current provision to be clearly understood YES - a copy of the Schedule of Services is attached, providing full cost breakdown:
  • Speech & Language Therapy: £64,780
  • Educational Psychology: £14,640
  • Counselling: £11,685
  • Resource bundle: Included (no charge)
Total annual cost: £91,105 (excluding VAT)
Public procurement rules complied with YES - this is a Direct Award on the following grounds:

Schedule 5, Clause 25 (Single Suppliers):
"The final justification is where, due to an absence of competition for technical reasons and provided there are no reasonable alternatives, only a particular supplier can supply the goods, services or works required."

Words First Limited offers an integrated multidisciplinary approach that combines multiple therapy services under one provider, ensuring:
  • Coordinated care across therapeutic disciplines
  • Consistent professional standards and safeguarding protocols
  • Seamless communication between therapists
  • Unified case management and progress tracking
This integrated model is not readily available from multiple separate providers without compromising coordination and effectiveness.
Compliant Tender undertaken Direct Procurement as per above justification under The Procurement Act 2023, Schedule 5, Clause 25.
Robust evaluation against criteria set up YES - Services have been evaluated against:
  • The specific therapeutic needs profile of Selwyn Primary School students
  • Volume of students requiring specialist intervention across multiple disciplines
  • The requirement for coordinated multidisciplinary support
  • Quality assurance and professional standards compliance
  • Value for money considerations including single-contract efficiency
  • Provider track record and professional qualifications
The evaluation confirms that Words First Limited's integrated service model meets all identified criteria and represents optimal value for the required scope of provision.

Note: All services are provided subject to Words First Limited's Data Protection Policy and Safer Recruitment Policy. Services commence from 1st September 2025 (or upon approval) and billing begins only once services have started at the school.
