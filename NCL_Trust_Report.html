Key Insights for Governors

✓ Strengths

  • Overall Absence Performance: All four schools perform at or better than the national average (8.9%), with Rokeby leading at 7.8%
  • Strong Performance Despite Context: Schools achieve good attendance despite serving highly deprived communities (FSM rates 2× national average)
  • Rokeby's Excellence: Best overall absence rate in the trust at 7.8%, very close to LA target of 7%

⚠ Areas for Development

  • Persistent Absence: All schools significantly above LA target (21%)
    • Eastlea: 27% (highest concern - RED rating)
    • Lister: 25.5%
    • Sarah Bonnell: 25.2%
    • Rokeby: 24.5% (best in trust, but still above target)
  • Eastlea Requires Focus: Smallest school (689 pupils) with highest persistent absence - may need targeted intervention

📊 Context & Observations

  • High Deprivation Context: All schools serve communities with FSM rates of 54-57% (nearly double national 28.9%)
  • Exceptional Linguistic Diversity: Rokeby has 81.3% EAL pupils (over 4× national average), requiring significant additional support
  • Single-Sex School Pattern: Sarah Bonnell (girls) and Rokeby (boys) both show notably lower SEN rates than mixed schools
  • Size Variation: Significant range from 588 (Rokeby) to 1,497 (Lister) pupils may impact resource allocation and economies of scale

Strategic Recommendations

  1. Persistent Absence Strategy: Develop trust-wide intervention program targeting the 21% LA benchmark, with priority support for Eastlea
  2. Share Best Practice: Investigate Rokeby's approaches to overall attendance (7.8%) for trust-wide implementation
  3. EAL Support Review: Assess whether Rokeby's exceptional EAL population (81.3%) has adequate resource allocation
  4. Eastlea Support: Consider additional resources for smallest school with highest persistent absence
  5. Celebrate Success: Recognize strong overall absence performance across the trust despite challenging context
