Newham Community Learning - Secondary Schools Exam Results 2024/25

This report presents the 2024/25 exam results for NCL's four secondary schools: Sarah Bonnell School, Rokeby School, Lister Community School, and Eastlea Community School. Data is from the DfE Compare Schools service, published October 2025. All comparisons include Local Authority (Newham) and England averages.

Context & Key Observations

  • Performance Spread: Significant variation across schools - Attainment 8 ranges from 41.4 (Eastlea) to 56.6 (Sarah Bonnell), spanning 15.2 points
  • Girls' School Excellence: Sarah Bonnell (56.6) significantly outperforms all schools, achieving 5.5 points above LA average
  • Eastlea Challenge: Eastlea faces substantial challenges with Grade 5+ performance at 27.0% (26.1 points below LA average of 53.1%)
  • EBacc Entry Paradox: Lister has highest EBacc entry (86.4%) but third-lowest Attainment 8 (44.1), suggesting potential curriculum appropriateness issues
  • Destinations Strength: Three of four schools meet or exceed national averages for staying in education/employment post-16
  • Boys' School Performance: Both Rokeby (50.6) and Eastlea (41.4) perform below LA average, with Eastlea requiring urgent intervention
  • No Progress 8 Data: Unable to assess value-added due to missing KS2 baseline data for 2024/25 and 2025/26 cohorts

Strategic Recommendations

  1. Eastlea Urgent Intervention: Immediate and comprehensive support required - Grade 5+ performance at 27.0% is 26.1 percentage points below LA average. Commission external review of curriculum delivery, teaching quality, assessment practices, and student support systems.
  2. Lister Curriculum Review: Despite highest EBacc entry (86.4%), Lister achieves only 44.1 Attainment 8 and 38.6% Grade 5+ English & Maths. Review appropriateness of universal EBacc pathway and consider differentiated curriculum routes.
  3. Share Sarah Bonnell Excellence: Establish formal mechanisms to share successful strategies from Sarah Bonnell (56.6 Attainment 8, 62.0% Grade 5+) across the trust, particularly in English and Maths departments.
  4. Boys' Schools Strategy: Both boys' schools underperform compared to Sarah Bonnell. Investigate gender-specific pedagogical approaches, behavioral strategies, and aspirations work that could close this gap.
  5. EBacc Entry Strategy: Rokeby (41.4%) and Eastlea (38.0%) significantly below LA average (59.2%) for EBacc entry. Analyze barriers including student aspirations, staffing capacity, and curriculum pathways.
  6. Rokeby Destinations Concern: Rokeby's 89% staying in education/employment is 3 points below LA average. Investigate careers guidance, post-16 pathways, and transition support.
  7. Trust-Wide English & Maths Focus: Grade 5+ rates vary from 27.0% to 62.0% across schools. Establish subject networks, standardize quality assurance, and implement evidence-based interventions.
  8. Progress 8 Preparation: Plan for reintroduction of Progress 8 measures. Ensure robust KS2 data tracking, intervention strategies, and value-added analysis systems are in place.
Note: Progress 8 scores are not available for 2024/25 and 2025/26 academic years due to missing KS2 baseline data caused by Covid-19 disruption. This means we cannot currently assess schools' value-added contribution. Attainment measures show final outcomes but not the progress journey from KS2 starting points. This particularly impacts our understanding of school effectiveness, as raw attainment scores are heavily influenced by prior attainment and intake characteristics.
